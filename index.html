  • The original 18 sample reports are synthetic; no actual Phnom Penh waste conditions are inferred.
  • Data is stored in this browser only. It is not shared, encrypted production storage or a multi-user database.
  • Photos use IndexedDB locally, with a 2 MB limit per image; clearing browser data may delete them.
  • OpenStreetMap tiles require internet access. The application data and analysis work without tiles.
  • Priority weights and factor normalization are provisional assumptions requiring stakeholder validation.
  • Location sensitivity is an explicitly entered estimate; schools, hospitals and waterways are not automatically verified.
  • DBSCAN can merge nearby dense groups through chains of core points. A cluster's diameter can therefore exceed 100 m.
  • The prototype does not dispatch trucks, guarantee collection, represent a government platform or use AI image classification.
  • Real deployment needs field-verified data, privacy safeguards, accessibility testing, secure shared backend and operational partnerships.
